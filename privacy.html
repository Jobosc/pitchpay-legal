PitchPay is an app for football teams to keep track of their penalty pot: players, penalties, trainings and matches, and who has paid. This policy explains which data the app processes and why.

2. Data we process

DataPurposeLegal basis (GDPR)
Email address and password (stored only as a hash)Your account and signing inArt. 6(1)(b) – contract

The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking, and does not use the advertising ID or your location. Data is also stored on your phone so the app works offline.

3. Who can see your data

Club data is only visible to members of that club. Members with the role "player" only see paid penalties and their own open penalties. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Service providers

We use these processors, bound by data processing agreements:

  • Supabase Inc. (USA) – database, sign-in and photo storage. Server region: [SUPABASE REGION, e.g. EU (Frankfurt)].
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC, USA) – delivering push notifications.
  • Apple Push Notification service (Apple Inc., USA) – delivering notifications on iPhone.

Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

5. How long we keep data

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Club data is kept as long as the club exists; an admin can delete the club at any time, which removes all its data and photos. See Delete your account for details.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. Children

PitchPay is intended for users aged 16 and over.
